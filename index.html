About Us

Dedicated Care for
Every Vessel We Serve

Buoyancy Elite Yacht Management was founded on a simple principle: every yacht owner deserves the same peace of mind that comes with having a trusted, hands-on professional watching over their investment. We deliver exactly that — on Lake Michigan and across Chicago's finest harbors.

Our team brings deep, hands-on experience with luxury motor yachts and classic vessels alike. We understand the systems, the materials, and the standards required to keep a fine vessel in showroom condition. From weekly washes to complex project coordination, we handle every detail with discretion and precision.

What sets us apart is consistency. You receive professional communication after every service visit, a clear record of what was done, and the confidence that your yacht will be exactly as you left it — or better.

What We Offer

Full-Service Yacht Care,
Start to Finish

Weekly Washing & Detailing

Regular hull and topside washes, gelcoat polish, and full exterior detailing to keep your vessel gleaming all season.

Interior Cleaning & Turnover

Full cabin cleaning, linen and towel turnover, galley sanitation, and odor control — so every trip begins in comfort.

Water Tank Top-Offs

Fresh water tank refilling and routine monitoring so you're always provisioned and ready to cast off.

Fender & Line Inspections

Routine inspection and adjustment of dock lines, fenders, and chafe gear to protect your hull at the dock.

Teak Restoration & Sealing

Expert teak cleaning, brightening, and sealing using premium products that restore the warm, honey-gold appearance of original teak.

Preventative Maintenance Checks

Systematic visual inspections of bilge, through-hulls, batteries, hoses, and mechanical systems to catch issues before they escalate.

Project Management

End-to-end oversight of refit, repair, and upgrade projects — from sourcing quotes to supervising completion and sign-off.

Vendor Coordination

We manage relationships with marine mechanics, riggers, canvas shops, and detailers on your behalf, coordinating access and quality control.

Captain Services

Licensed, experienced captains available for day charters, delivery runs, sea trials, or owner-accompanied trips on Lake Michigan.

Seasonal Care Programs

Comprehensive season-long packages covering commissioning, weekly care, and winterization — fully tailored to your vessel and schedule.

Our Approach

Peace of Mind Is
the Standard, Not a Perk

We believe exceptional yacht care is built on three pillars: consistency, communication, and craftsmanship. Every service visit follows a structured protocol designed to leave nothing to chance — and every visit ends with a detailed report to you.

We take ownership of your vessel's appearance and mechanical readiness as if it were our own. That means proactively flagging concerns, coordinating with specialists when needed, and maintaining meticulous service records throughout the season.

The result: a yacht that's always ready for an impulsive evening departure, always in better condition than when you left it, and an investment that's protected for seasons to come.
