About Us

Dedicated Care for
Every Vessel We Serve

Buoyancy Elite was founded on a simple principle: every yacht owner deserves the peace of mind that comes with having a trusted, hands-on professional watching over their investment — on Lake Michigan and across Chicago's finest harbors.

We bring deep experience with luxury motor yachts and classic vessels alike. From weekly washes to complex project coordination, we handle every detail with discretion and precision. You receive professional communication after every visit and a meticulous record of what was done.

What We Offer

Full-Service Yacht Care,
Start to Finish

Weekly Washing & Detailing

Regular hull and topside washes, gelcoat polish, and full exterior detailing to keep your vessel gleaming all season.

Interior Cleaning & Turnover

Full cabin cleaning, linen and towel turnover, galley sanitation, and odor control — so every trip begins in comfort.

Water Tank Top-Offs

Fresh water tank refilling and routine monitoring so you're always provisioned and ready to cast off.

Fender & Line Inspections

Routine inspection and adjustment of dock lines, fenders, and chafe gear to protect your hull at the dock.

Teak Restoration & Sealing

Expert teak cleaning, brightening, and sealing using premium products that restore the warm honey-gold appearance of original teak.

Preventative Maintenance

Systematic visual inspections of bilge, through-hulls, batteries, hoses, and mechanical systems to catch issues before they escalate.

Project Management

End-to-end oversight of refit, repair, and upgrade projects — from sourcing quotes to supervising completion and sign-off.

Vendor Coordination

We manage relationships with marine mechanics, riggers, canvas shops, and detailers on your behalf.

Captain Services

Licensed, experienced captains available for day charters, delivery runs, sea trials, or owner-accompanied trips on Lake Michigan.

Seasonal Care Programs

Comprehensive season-long packages covering commissioning, weekly care, and winterization — fully tailored to your vessel.

Our Approach

Peace of Mind Is
the Standard, Not a Perk

We believe exceptional yacht care is built on three pillars: consistency, communication, and craftsmanship. Every service visit follows a structured protocol — and every visit ends with a detailed report.

We take ownership of your vessel's appearance and mechanical readiness as if it were our own. Proactively flagging concerns, coordinating with specialists, and maintaining meticulous service records throughout the season.

Seasonal Programs

A Full Season of
Effortless Ownership

From spring commissioning to fall haul-out and winterization, we manage every aspect of the season so you can simply enjoy time on the water.

  • Spring commissioning — systems check, polish, and launch readiness
  • Weekly maintenance visits throughout the sailing season
  • Pre-departure readiness checks before every outing
  • Mid-season teak and brightwork service
  • Fall haul-out coordination and full winterization
  • Detailed season-end condition report and recommendations
