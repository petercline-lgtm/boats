SEC. 04

A season on the lake, managed end to end

Chicago Park District harbors run roughly April through November.

Apr — May
Commissioning
Launch coordination, bottom & hull detail, systems inspection and de-winterization, safety gear check, shakedown run. The boat meets the season ready — not catching up to it.
May — Oct
Active Season
Weekly care visits and logs, interior turnovers, tank top-offs, preventative inspections, storm checks after every blow, captains and provisioning on 48 hours' notice.
Oct — Nov
Decommissioning
Haul-out coordination, engine and systems winterization, shrink-wrap, batteries to tender, and a documented lay-up report so spring starts from a known state.
Dec — Mar
Winter Watch
Monthly storage checks, cover and cradle inspection after heavy weather, and refit or upgrade projects supervised in the yard while you're away — finished before launch, not after.

NOTE Roughly 180 days of boating weather a year. Our job is making sure none of them are spent waiting on a vendor.

SEC. 06

Owners' questions

The ones we hear at the dock, answered straight.

Q.1What does a weekly visit actually include?+

A full wash of hull and topsides, interior walkthrough and turnover, fresh-water top-off, line and fender inspection, and a bilge, battery, and through-hull check — closed out with a photographed care log the same day. Your program may add teak care, canvas work, or provisioning.

Q.2Do you replace my mechanic, or work with him?+

We work with him. If you have a mechanic, rigger, or canvas shop you trust, we coordinate and supervise them. If you don't, we bring vendors we've worked with for years and stand behind. Either way, you make one call — to us.

Q.3What happens when a storm comes through?+

Every vessel on a standing program gets a dock check within 24 hours of sustained winds over 25 knots — lines, fenders, cover, bilge. You get the all-clear by text, or a phone call if something needs a decision.

Q.4Are you insured, and are your captains licensed?+

Fully insured — documentation available on request, and we're happy to be named on your policy's requirements. Every captain we place holds a current USCG license with Great Lakes experience.

Q.5Can you handle work over the winter?+

Winter is when the big work should happen. We scope projects in the fall, supervise the yard through the off-season with weekly photo reports, and sea-trial the result before launch — so your spring isn't spent waiting on a punch list.

Q.6What does a program cost?+

It depends on the vessel, the harbor, and what you want covered — which is why we start with a free dockside walkthrough rather than a rate sheet. You'll have a written proposal within one business day of stepping aboard.
