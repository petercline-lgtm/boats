01 The Ethos

Ownership, the way
the brochure promised.

Buoyancy Elite was founded on a simple conviction: a yacht should give back more time than it takes. Yet most owners spend their weekends chasing vendors, checking lines after a blow, and wondering when the teak was last oiled.

We take all of it. A dedicated crew visits your vessel every week — washing, inspecting, topping off, turning over the interior — and documents every call with a photographed care log. When you walk down the dock on Friday evening, the only thing left to do is cast off.

Quiet, meticulous, and always a step ahead.

06 Questions

Asked,
answered.

Anything else on your mind? Ask us directly

Which harbors do you serve?+

All nine Chicago Park District harbors — Belmont, Burnham, Diversey, DuSable, Monroe, Montrose, 31st Street, Jackson Park, and 59th Street — plus select North Shore clubs and Indiana marinas by arrangement.

What does weekly care actually include?+

A full wash of hull and topsides, interior walkthrough, fresh-water tank top-off, line and fender inspection, a bilge and systems glance, and anything your vessel's program adds — all closed out with a photographed care log the same day.

Can you oversee projects while I'm away?+

Yes — refits, repairs, and upgrades are managed end to end: scoping, competitive quotes, vendor supervision, weekly progress reports with photos, and final sign-off before we hand her back.

Are you insured?+

Fully insured, and every captain we place is USCG-licensed. Documentation and references are available on request.

How do I know what was done?+

After every visit you receive a timestamped care log — photos, notes, anything we found, and anything we recommend. No mystery invoices, no "trust us."

What does a program cost?+

Every program is tailored to the vessel, the harbor, and the season you have in mind. Tell us about your boat and you'll have a written proposal within one business day.
