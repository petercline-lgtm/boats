The Work

I.

Weekly Stewardship

Hull and topside washes, gelcoat polish, brightwork care, fender and line inspection, and fresh-water tank top-offs — every visit closed with a photographed care log sent directly to you.

II.

Interior Care & Turnover

Full cabin detail, linen and towel turnover, galley sanitation, and odor control. Every trip begins in a vessel that has been prepared, not merely cleaned.

III.

Teak Restoration & Sealing

Two-part cleaning, brightening, and premium sealing using products chosen for Lake Michigan's specific conditions. The warm honey-gold appearance of original teak, preserved.

IV.

Preventative Maintenance

Systematic inspection of bilge, through-hulls, batteries, hoses, and mechanical systems on every visit. Issues found early cost a fraction of those found late.

V.

Project & Refit Management

End-to-end oversight of repair, upgrade, and refit projects — from scoping and competitive quotes through vendor supervision, weekly progress reports, and final sign-off.

VI.

Vendor Coordination

We manage your relationships with marine mechanics, riggers, canvas shops, electronics specialists, and detailers. One call to us handles all of them.

VII.

Captain Services

USCG-licensed, experienced captains available for day charters, delivery runs, sea trials, and owner-accompanied passages on Lake Michigan.

VIII.

Seasonal Programs

Comprehensive season-long care covering spring commissioning, weekly maintenance throughout the season, and full winterization — tailored to your vessel and how you use it.

The Waters

A season on
Lake Michigan
is short.

From ice-out in April through the November haul, the Great Lakes sailing season lasts roughly 180 days. Every one of them matters. We treat the window between first thaw and first snow as a gift — and make sure nothing is wasted waiting on a vendor or chasing down a problem that should have been caught in May.

We commission your vessel before the season opens, watch over it through every storm and calm between, and put it away properly when the water goes cold. You own the season. We manage it.

April — May

Commissioning

Launch coordination, full detail, systems inspection, and a shakedown before you're aboard. Your boat meets the season ready — not catching up to it.

May — October

Active Stewardship

Weekly care, storm watches, interior turnovers, tank top-offs, and concierge readiness — all season, every week, documented every time.

October — November

Winterization

Haul-out, engine and systems winterization, shrink-wrap, and a documented winter plan — including any refit work you want completed before spring.
